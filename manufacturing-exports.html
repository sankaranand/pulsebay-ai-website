WHERE AI CREATES VALUE

From enquiry to dispatch, we focus on the work that slows operations down.

AI connects information, automates repetitive steps and surfaces exceptions across the systems and channels your teams already use.

01

Sales & order management

Capture enquiries from email, WhatsApp and portals, extract order details, prepare responses and reduce re-entry into ERP or Tally.

02

Procurement & supplier follow-up

Track purchase orders, chase suppliers, summarise status and surface delivery exceptions before they become operational problems.

03

Inventory & planning

Bring demand, stock and open orders together to support reorder decisions, planning reviews and exception handling.

04

Production & dispatch

Connect order information, production status and dispatch activities so teams spend less time reconciling updates across systems.

05

Quality & compliance documents

Classify documents, extract key information, track validity and route exceptions for inspection, audit and renewal activities.

06

Business knowledge & management

Turn scattered operational data and documents into trusted answers, summaries, alerts and management briefings with source traceability.

WHAT A FIRST WORKFLOW LOOKS LIKE

Example: capturing customer orders without re-typing.

One of the most common starting points. AI does the reading, extracting and checking, while your team stays in control.

STEP 1
Order arrives

By email, WhatsApp, PDF purchase order or buyer portal.

STEP 2
AI extracts

Customer, items, quantities, rates and delivery dates.

STEP 3
AI checks

Against price lists, item masters, stock and past orders.

STEP 4
Your team approves

A ready draft to confirm, with mismatches flagged rather than guessed.

STEP 5
Posted to ERP / Tally

Acknowledgement drafted to the customer; status tracked through dispatch.

HUMAN CONTROL, BY DESIGN

How we decide what AI is allowed to do.

Not every process needs the same level of autonomy. We don't automate simply because it's technically possible.

LEVEL 1
Assist

AI drafts, summarises or prepares information. Your team approves.

LEVEL 2
Recommend

AI flags a likely action or exception. A person decides.

LEVEL 3
Automate

AI executes routine steps. Exceptions route to a person.

LEVEL 4
Agentic

AI runs an end-to-end workflow, but only where the process is predictable, low-risk and measurable.

Our principle

Autonomy increases only as confidence, controls and business value are demonstrated.

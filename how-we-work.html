HOW WE DECIDE

Not every process needs the same level of AI autonomy.

We increase autonomy only when confidence, controls and business value are demonstrated.

LEVEL 1
Assist

AI drafts, summarises or prepares information. A person approves.

LEVEL 2
Recommend

AI identifies a likely action or exception. A person decides.

LEVEL 3
Automate

AI executes routine steps. Exceptions route to a person.

LEVEL 4
Agentic

AI executes an end-to-end workflow only where it is predictable, low-risk and measurable.

Human control is designed into the workflow.

Quality, compliance, finance, production and other higher-risk activities receive stronger approval, monitoring and exception handling. We do not automate simply because automation is technically possible.
